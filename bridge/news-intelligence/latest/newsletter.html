Anthropic faces enterprise spending headwinds

What happened: Corporate spending on Anthropic’s flagship Fable 5 model has plateaued at 11% of total outlay on Anthropic tools, as businesses increasingly pivot to cheaper alternatives.

Why it matters: It highlights a growing price sensitivity among enterprise customers, suggesting that performance alone may not sustain premium pricing in a maturing AI market.

Our read: Our read is that the 'AI gold rush' phase is transitioning into a 'cost-optimization' phase, where businesses are prioritizing budget efficiency over top-tier model capabilities.

US AI infrastructure investment outpaces Europe 3x

What happened: Oxford Economics projects US corporate spending on equipment and facilities will rise 40% between 2021 and 2027, a rate more than three times faster than European growth.

Why it matters: This divergence in capital expenditure indicates a widening gap in the foundational infrastructure required to support large-scale AI deployment between the two regions.

Our read: Our read is that the US is aggressively front-loading its AI transition, potentially creating a long-term competitive advantage in compute and data center capacity that Europe may struggle to bridge.
