## Story comparison

### Could disclosure become part of the creator workflow?

- Audience: Creators, product teams, compliance leads and marketing ops teams shipping AI-assisted content
- Interest signal: A concrete policy date plus an operational question that affects any public-facing AI output
- Stop reason: The question is immediately legible: teams have to place disclosure somewhere in the workflow before content ships
- Payoff: A simple way to map one output path and decide where the label or review step lives
- Proof: European Commission guidance on AI Act transparency duties beginning 2 August 2026, including user disclosure and machine-readable marking
- Overall score: 9/10
- Reason: Strongest mix of confirmed policy change, clear operational consequence and easy 30-second explanation.
