## Overall reasoning

The strategy focuses on the most critical pain point for freelancers: the legal and operational risk of using AI for client work. By emphasizing that 'reading AI work twice is not the same as knowing what to check,' we position the Clearforge product as the essential missing link in their workflow. The TikTok script is kept under 30 seconds and 55 words to ensure high retention, while other platforms provide more depth to support professional decision-making.
