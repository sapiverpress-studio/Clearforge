## Overall reasoning

The strategy focuses on the immediate, practical needs of freelancers: data privacy (via self-hosting) and regulatory compliance (via the EU AI Act). By framing the content around the 'pre-send' moment, we address the core problem that viewers leave early because they don't see the immediate relevance of the content to their daily work. The tone is kept calm, professional, and evidence-based, avoiding hype and focusing on the 'how-to' of auditing AI output.
