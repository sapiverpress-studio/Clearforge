## TikTok Script

Most students and parents are using AI completely wrong for study prep. The secret isn't asking ChatGPT to write your essay or solve your math homework. That actually weakens your memory and writing skills. Instead, use AI as a study scaffold. Upload your actual class notes or textbook PDFs into a grounded tool like Gemini Notebook or ChatGPT Edu. Then, prompt it to build a 5-question multiple-choice practice quiz or a clean mind map based ONLY on your uploaded file. You get instant active-recall practice, zero false facts, and your brain still does the real learning.
