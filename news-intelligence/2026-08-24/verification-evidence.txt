* **What is confirmed:** New Zealand Prime Minister Christopher Luxon and Education Minister Erica Stanford officially announced on August 24, 2026, the introduction of the *Online Safety (Minimum Age and Child Safety Risk Assessment) Bill*. The bill proposes banning under-16s from high-risk social media platforms (including Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and Facebook). It mandates age verification measures (such as facial age estimation, digital identity documents, or account data) and imposes non-compliance penalties on tech platforms of up to 10% of global revenue.
* **Important caveats:** The bill does **not** currently have a guaranteed majority to pass into law. Luxon’s coalition partners—the libertarian ACT Party and Winston Peters’ New Zealand First—have announced they will vote against the measure. Its passage depends on whether the opposition Labour Party decides to support it.
* **Publication status:** **Suitable for publication today.** (Major breaking international policy story).
