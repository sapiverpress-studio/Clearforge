#### **Candidate [10]: California Legislature passes AB 1856 exempting open-source OSes from age-verification law**
* **Confirmed:** The California Legislature unanimously passed Assembly Bill 1856 (Assembly 69-0, Senate 39-0). The bill amends 2025’s Digital Age Assurance Act (AB 1043) by redefining "operating system provider" to explicitly exempt open-source operating systems distributed under permissive or copyleft licenses (such as GPL, MIT, BSD, and Apache). This excludes Linux distributions (Debian, Fedora, Ubuntu) and BSD projects from mandatory account-setup age verification. Commercial proprietary OSes (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android) remain in scope. The bill now awaits Governor Gavin Newsom's signature.
* **Caveats:** Civil liberties groups (e.g., EFF) note that while AB 1856 resolves open-source compliance fears, it expands age-gating mandates for web browsers and online platform operators.
* **Publication Suitability:** **Suitable for publication today.**
