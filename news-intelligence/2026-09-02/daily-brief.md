## 2. OpenAI integrates ChatGPT Health with Epic EHR systems

**Why it matters:** This integration provides clinicians with direct access to AI-assisted data synthesis within established healthcare workflows, provided they operate under a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The read-only constraint highlights a cautious approach to clinical safety, prioritising information retrieval over automated documentation generation.

## 3. ECB warns of AI-driven tech debt in European credit markets

**Confirmed:** An ECB blog post reports that US tech firms account for nearly 10% of new corporate bond issuance in the eurozone this year, totalling approximately €40 billion.

**Why it matters:** The concentration of debt from a few large tech companies to fund AI infrastructure may increase borrowing costs and crowd out other European corporate borrowers.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The analysis suggests that the scale of AI capital expenditure is now significant enough to influence regional credit market dynamics beyond the technology sector itself.

**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/us-tech-firms-may-crowd-out-others-euro-bond-market-raise-credit-risk-2026-09-02/) · confidence 85%
