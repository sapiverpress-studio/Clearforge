**Why it matters:** This architectural shift may complicate safety monitoring, as it bypasses the readable chain-of-thought text that researchers currently use to audit AI reasoning.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The move toward opaque, high-efficiency reasoning techniques highlights a potential tension between optimizing model performance and maintaining transparency for safety and alignment.

## 4. NYC Department of Education Restricts AI in Schools

**Confirmed:** The New York City Department of Education has implemented a policy banning AI companion chatbots across all grades and restricting general AI tool usage for students below high school level.

**Why it matters:** This policy represents a significant regulatory hurdle for AI developers targeting the education sector, forcing a re-evaluation of how AI tools are deployed in classroom environments.

**Sapiver Forge interpretation:** The restriction reflects growing institutional caution regarding the impact of AI companions on student development and data privacy in public education.
