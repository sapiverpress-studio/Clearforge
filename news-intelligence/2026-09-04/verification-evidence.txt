#### **Candidate [9]: Anthropic Claude Formalizes Proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean 4**
* **Confirmed:** Anthropic published research demonstrating that Claude worked largely autonomously over 11 days using the *Prove2Me* platform to write a complete Lean 4 computer-checked formalization of Andrew Wiles' proof of Fermat's Last Theorem. The codebase spans ~13 million lines of Lean code and proves over 29,000 sub-theorems. Independent mathematicians compiled the code and confirmed it passed Lean's automated type-checker.
* **Important Caveats:** The formalization relied on Andrew Wiles' existing human proof structure rather than inventing a new proof. Complete peer review of the massive 13M-line code structure by human mathematicians will take considerable time.
* **Suitable for Publication Today:** **YES.** Landmark milestone for AI in formal logic and mathematical reasoning.
