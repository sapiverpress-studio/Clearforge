# Output Release Gate 30-Day Commercial Validation

Create one useful, source-grounded content package for freelancers, creators or small agencies who are about to send, publish or deliver AI-assisted work.

Use only a current retrieved source that supports one of these release problems: unsupported claims; confidentiality or personal information; rights, ownership or disclosure; wrong version, destination or recipient; automated actions or handoffs; suitability and human judgement.

The article must follow this order:

1. A plain-language, problem-led headline.
2. A recognisable customer situation.
3. The verified external evidence.
4. Separate sections or unmistakable wording for what the source proves, what Sapiver Forge infers, and what remains unknown.
5. The practical consequence for the target customer.
6. Three to five checks before release.
7. A short, accurate explanation of the Output Release Gate as a structured human review tool for the exact output.
8. One direct product call to action.

Social assets must lead with the customer problem, give one useful check and use the same single product call to action. Do not turn the package into a general news summary. Do not force an unsuitable source into the campaign.
