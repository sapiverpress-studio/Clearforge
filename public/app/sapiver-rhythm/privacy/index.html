Sapiver Rhythm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Sapiver Rhythm is a two-hand rhythm training app published by Sapiver Press. The trainer is designed to work locally on the user’s Android device. No Sapiver Rhythm account is required.

Data stored on the device

Sapiver Rhythm stores a small amount of practice state locally on the device so the app can remember the user’s current level, unlocked progress, selected tempo and timing-accuracy preference. This information is stored in the app’s local storage and is not sent to Sapiver Press.

Practice input and timing

The app responds to taps on the left-hand and right-hand practice pads and uses their timing to provide immediate practice feedback. Tap timing and per-attempt accuracy results are processed on the device and are not uploaded to Sapiver Press.

Network use, analytics and advertising

The core Sapiver Rhythm trainer does not require an account, cloud synchronisation or a Sapiver Press server. The app does not contain Sapiver Press advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking, and Sapiver Press does not collect or sell personal data through the trainer.

Permissions

Sapiver Rhythm does not require microphone recording for rhythm practice. The Android build explicitly blocks microphone-recording permission. The app does not require access to contacts, location or the camera for its core functionality.

Privacy-policy link

If the user chooses to open this privacy policy from the app, the policy is opened using the device’s normal web-link handling. The browser or network provider used to open the page may process ordinary connection information under its own terms. Sapiver Rhythm does not use that visit to build a user profile.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored practice state remains on the device until it is replaced by later practice state, the app’s storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Because Sapiver Press does not receive that local practice state, there is no remote Sapiver Rhythm account or server-side practice record for Sapiver Press to delete.

Children

Sapiver Rhythm is a general music-practice tool for beginner and developing musicians. It is not designed specifically for children and does not include social networking, advertising or account creation.

Changes to this policy

If Sapiver Rhythm’s data handling changes materially, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app release and the Google Play Data safety information will be kept consistent with it.

Contact

Privacy enquiries about Sapiver Rhythm can be made through Sapiver Press at suite.sapiverpress.co.uk.
