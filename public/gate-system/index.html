Sapiver Forge Applied AI Gate System

Human checkpoints for consequential AI-assisted work.

Sapiver Forge provides practical local-first tools for creators, freelancers and small teams that need clearer decisions before AI is introduced, while it operates, before output leaves and after results can be measured.

Choose the right GateView the complete system

The four decisions

  1. Opportunity Gate

    Should AI be considered for this task?

    A practical decision tool for testing whether AI is suitable, worthwhile and appropriately bounded before time, money or sensitive information is committed.

  2. Workflow Control Gate

    Is the proposed workflow properly bounded and ready to operate?

    A practical control tool for defining ownership, boundaries, approval points, evidence requirements and fallback routes before an AI-assisted workflow begins operating.

  3. Output Release Gate

    Is this exact consequential output ready to leave?

    A structured final checkpoint for checking evidence, names, dates, figures, links, privacy, ownership, disclosure and release readiness before AI-assisted work is published, sent or used.

  4. Outcome Review Gate

    Is the operating workflow delivering worthwhile results?

    A practical review tool for comparing expected and actual outcomes and deciding whether to continue, change, pause or retire an AI-assisted workflow.

What the tools are

Structured human decision support.

They make checks, evidence, ownership and decisions easier to record and repeat.

What the tools are not

Automated approval or legal advice.

They do not guarantee accuracy, safety, compliance or successful outcomes.
