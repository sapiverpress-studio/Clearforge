Build your own with AI

Copy this prompt, then change the names, rewards or rules to fit your family:

Build me a mobile-friendly family job board as one standalone HTML file. Let a parent add a job, assign a child's name and set a reward. A child can mark a job finished, but only a separate grown-up approval button adds it to that child's balance. Include available, awaiting-check and approved views, per-child unpaid totals, a paid button, deletion with confirmation and localStorage persistence. Use no external libraries and explain privacy accurately.

Privacy: Names, jobs and balances stay in this browser's local storage. This page does not send them to Sapiver Press or another service. Clearing browser data will remove them. This is a family record, not a banking or payment service.
