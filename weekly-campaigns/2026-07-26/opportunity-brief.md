## Genuine audience problem

### AI outputs are easy to generate, but hard to ship safely

Solo creators and small businesses are increasingly using AI for notes, drafts, support replies, design, and admin, but they often lack a simple system for review, provenance, and handoff before the output is shared or acted on.

- **Affected audience:** Solo creators, freelancers, small business owners, and small teams using AI for content, coordination, support, and lightweight operations.
- **Practical consequence:** AI saves time on the first draft, then creates rework, uncertainty, or risk when nobody has defined the human checkpoint, logging step, or disclosure rule.
- **Why now:** This week’s approved research shows AI moving deeper into meetings, bookkeeping, workflow boards, search, video creation, ad disclosure, and enterprise deployment, while transparency and control features are becoming more visible.
- **Evergreen value:** Every AI workflow still needs a point where a human checks the output, decides what happens next, and knows how the work was generated.

## Recommended product

### AI Workflow Handoff Kit

**Promise:** Turn an AI-assisted task into a reviewable workflow with a clear handoff, a human checkpoint, and a simple log before anything is sent, published, or acted on.

**Target customer:** Solo creators and small businesses that use AI for content, admin, support, research, or lightweight operations.

**Outcome:** Fewer AI drafts that stall, fewer accidental sends, clearer provenance, and a repeatable way to test whether AI actually saves time.

**Minimum viable contents:**
- Workflow map template: input, output, owner, handoff, and stop point
- Human review gate checklist for publish, send, and approve moments
- Prompt/output log sheet to capture what the AI produced and what changed
- Disclosure and provenance checklist for public-facing or client-facing work
- One-week pilot scorecard to measure time saved versus cleanup time

- **Worked example:** A creator uses Canva AI 2.0 to draft a social graphic. The kit forces them to define the brief, note the brand rules, mark the human review step, log the changes made after the AI draft, and decide whether the asset is ready to publish or needs another pass.
- **Suggested price:** £29
- **Main objection:** A checklist will not improve the model itself, and some workflows still need product-specific controls or legal review.
- **Demonstration:** Walk through one real task in five minutes: capture the brief, generate the first draft, apply the review gate, record the changes, and show the final release decision.
- **Bundle destination:** Clearforge AI at Work bundle

- Do not position the pack as legal, medical, or compliance advice.
- Keep the product tied to one workflow at a time; avoid suggesting broad AI transformation.
- Use clear disclosure language for any public-facing or client-facing AI output.
